Privacy Policy

Fleet Hound is operated by K2026453166 (SOUTH AFRICA) (PTY) LTD ("we", "us"), a company in South Africa. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use the Fleet Hound website, the Fleet Hound app, or a WhatsApp flow connected to Fleet Hound.

It covers customers and users in South Africa and Zambia. It is written for the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). If you are in Zambia, the Data Protection Act, 2021 may also apply to you.

Our role

When a fleet company uses Fleet Hound, that company decides why driver, vehicle, and trip information is collected. That company is the responsible party for that information. We store and process it on the company's instructions. In POPIA terms, we are the operator.

We are the responsible party for information we collect on this website, including demo requests, and for messages sent to our privacy contact.

Information we collect

Demo requests. The Request Demo form on this website asks for a first name, last name, work email, and an optional message. We save those details in our database so we can respond.

Accounts. The customer company, or its users, provide a first name, last name, username, email address, phone number, password, role, company, and region. Where the customer records them, we also store driver licence expiry, professional driving permit (PDP) expiry, and passport expiry.

Fleet records. Customers enter vehicles, odometer readings, engine hours, maintenance, refuelling, trips, locations, tolls, and expenses.

WhatsApp refuel flow. When a driver uses the WhatsApp flow, we receive the phone number presented with the flow and the refuel details submitted in it, such as volume, price, and odometer. We use the phone number to match the submission to a registered user.

Technical data. The app stores a login token, display name, user role, and active region in the browser's local storage so the user can stay signed in. Our server logs may include an IP address and the time of a request, which we use to run and protect the service.

We do not use advertising cookies or a third-party analytics product. The public website loads typefaces from Google Fonts and a styling library from the Tailwind content-delivery network. Those providers receive the IP address and browser details needed to deliver those files.

Why we use it

  • To provide the fleet management service the customer asked for.
  • To create and protect user accounts, including password resets.
  • To answer demo requests.
  • To match a WhatsApp refuel submission to a registered user.
  • To keep the service secure and to fix faults.
  • To meet a legal duty when one applies.

Who else handles it

We do not sell personal information.

DigitalOcean. Our server is a DigitalOcean virtual server in the United States. Personal information from the website, the app, and WhatsApp flows is stored there.

Meta (WhatsApp). When a driver uses a WhatsApp flow, Meta Platforms processes the phone number and the contents of the flow in order to deliver them to our server. We then store that information on our DigitalOcean server. We do not pass it on for anyone else's own use.

Proton Mail. Our contact inbox, wpnel@protonmail.com, is a Proton Mail address. Messages sent to that address, including privacy requests, are processed by Proton so we can read and reply to them. Service emails the app sends, such as a password reset, are sent from our server to the email address on the account.

Storage outside South Africa and Zambia

Because the server is in the United States, personal information from South Africa and Zambia is stored there. A WhatsApp flow also passes through Meta before it reaches us. We use this arrangement to host and provide Fleet Hound.

How long we keep it

We keep a customer's fleet and account information while that company is a Fleet Hound customer. We delete it within 60 days after the company ends that relationship, unless the law requires a record to be kept for longer.

We keep a demo request for 12 months from the date we receive it, then delete it, unless the person becomes a customer or asks us to delete it sooner.

Security

Access to the app requires a signed-in account. Passwords are stored in hashed form. Connections to the service use HTTPS. Access to the server is limited to the people who operate Fleet Hound.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask whether we hold personal information about you, ask for a record of it, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to processing or ask us to restrict it in the cases the Act allows. You may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

If your employer or another customer company added your information to Fleet Hound, contact that company first. We will help them respond to requests about information we process for them.

For a demo request, or for other information we hold as responsible party, email wpnel@protonmail.com.

If you are in Zambia, you may also contact the Data Protection Commissioner of Zambia about your rights under the Data Protection Act, 2021.

Children

Fleet Hound is a service for businesses. We do not seek personal information about children.

Changes
